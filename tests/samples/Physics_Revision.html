Buoyancy revision

Archimedes' principle states that the upward force on a submerged object is equal to the weight of the fluid it displaces.

Key ideas

  • Buoyant force = weight of displaced fluid
  • An object floats when its density is less than the fluid's
  • Pressure at depth: p = p0 + rho g h
