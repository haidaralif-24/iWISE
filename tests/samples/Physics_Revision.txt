BUOYANCY - QUICK REVISION NOTES

Archimedes' principle: the upward force on a submerged object is equal
to the weight of the fluid it displaces.

KEY IDEAS
1. Buoyant force = weight of displaced fluid
2. An object floats when its density is less than the fluid's
3. Pressure at depth: p = p0 + rho g h

WORKED EXAMPLE
A 1000 cm3 block of wood (density 600 kg/m3) is held underwater.
Fb = 1000 x 9.81 x 0.001 = 9.81 N
W  =  600 x 9.81 x 0.001 = 5.89 N
Net upward force = 3.92 N
